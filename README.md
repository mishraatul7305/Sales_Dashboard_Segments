# 📊 Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze sales performance, profit drivers, payment preferences, and customer segmentation. 

## 📈 Key Metrics & Highlights
* **Total Sales:** 1.57M
* **Total Profit:** 175.26K
* **Total Rows Analyzed:** 30M
* **Sales by Payment Mode:** 
  * COD: 0.67M[cite: 1, 2]
  * Online: 0.55M[cite: 1, 2]
  * Cards: 0.34M[cite: 1, 2]

## 🔍 Key Insights & Features
1. **AI-Powered Key Influencers:** Analyzed what drives sales to increase. Findings show that sales are significantly more likely to increase when the *Sum of Profit* exceeds 100.656 (averaging an increase of 897.4)[cite: 1, 2].
2. **Customer Segmentation:** Breakdown of sales across Consumer ($0.75M), Corporate ($0.51M), and Home Office ($0.30M) segments[cite: 1, 2
3. **Quarterly Trends:** Monitored revenue growth across quarters, peaking at Qtr 4 with 0.58M[cite: 1, 2].
4. **Top Segments Analysis:** Identified specific data segments and evaluated average sales against overall averages to pinpoint underperforming or high-value pockets[cite: 1, 2].
